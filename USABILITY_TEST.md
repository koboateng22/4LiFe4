# 4Life — quick usability test

A short test to run with **5–6 students** on their own phones. One session takes about 20 minutes. It checks that the three main features are easy and fast to use, and shows what to fix next.

## Before you start
- Use the demo account (Kwame Mensah, PIN 1234) so nobody shares personal information.
- Say: "We are testing the app, not you. Think out loud. There are no wrong answers."
- Do **not** help or point. Only read the task.
- Note the time each task takes and where people hesitate.

## Tasks and targets

| # | Task (read this out) | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You feel in danger. Call for emergency help." | 1 tap on Instant EMS; 2 from any other screen (SOS, then Call 112) |
| 2 | "Send your support person a message with where you are." | 2 taps from Instant EMS |
| 3 | "You have a strong craving. Get help riding it out." | 2 taps from Home |
| 4 | "Start the 20-minute craving timer." | under 30 seconds |
| 5 | "Write down a heavy thought and find a fairer one." | under 3 minutes |
| 6 | "Let someone's Village post know it helped you, without writing anything." | 1 tap |
| 7 | "Write a short Village post." | 3 taps to start writing |
| 8 | "Say you're going to the next group circle and set a reminder." | under 30 seconds |
| 9 | "Make your safety plan." | under 5 minutes |
| 10 | "Find the nearest hospital and get directions." | 2 taps from Instant EMS |

## After the tasks, ask
1. What was the easiest thing to do? What was the hardest?
2. Was anything too small to read or tap?
3. Did any screen feel crowded? Which one?
4. Would you use this on a hard evening? Why or why not?
5. How would you feel if a friend saw this app on your phone?

## What to write down
- Time per task, and whether the person finished it alone.
- Every place they paused, tapped the wrong thing, or said "where is…?".
- Their exact words (good and bad).

## After the sessions
- Fix anything that **2 or more** people struggled with.
- Re-run the tasks that failed with 2–3 new students.
